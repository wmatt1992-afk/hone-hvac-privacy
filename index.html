Privacy Policy — Hone HVAC PM Estimator

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Hone HVAC PM Estimator ("the App") is a tool for building HVAC preventive-maintenance contract estimates. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Summary

Everything you enter to build an estimate — customer information, equipment details, pricing, and photos — is stored only on the device you're using. The App has no server for your estimate data and does not upload your estimates, customers, or photos to us or anyone else. The one exception is the optional paid subscription: to sell and validate a subscription, the App uses Google Play's billing system and a subscription-management service (RevenueCat), which receive limited purchase and device information described below. Your estimate content is never included in that.

What information you enter into the App

When you create an estimate, you may enter:

  • Customer/contact information (company name, address, contact name, phone, email)
  • Site photos taken with your device's camera or selected from your photo library
  • Equipment and job details (equipment type, condition, service visits, notes)
  • Pricing and cost information (labor rates, margins, materials)

How this information is stored

  • All estimate data, including photos, is stored locally on your device (in on-device storage), not on a remote server.
  • The App does not transmit estimate data, customer information, or photos to us or to any third party.
  • Data stays on the device until you delete it, export it yourself (e.g., to send a file to a coworker), or uninstall the App.

Subscriptions and payments

The App offers an optional paid subscription. Subscriptions are purchased and billed through Google Play; Google processes your payment, and the App never receives or stores your payment card or billing details.

To determine whether you have an active subscription, the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. When you purchase, restore, or use a subscription, the following limited information may be sent to Google Play and RevenueCat:

  • Your purchase and subscription status (the product purchased, and whether it is active, renewed, or cancelled)
  • A purchase identifier/receipt from Google Play
  • An app-generated, anonymous identifier used to associate a purchase with your installation
  • Basic technical information such as app version, platform, and country/region

This information is used only to sell and validate your subscription and to restore purchases. Your estimate content — customer information, job details, pricing, and photos — is never included in or sent through this process. For how these providers handle data, see RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Camera and photo access

The App requests camera and photo-library access only so you can attach site photos to an estimate. Photos are used solely within the App and are stored with the estimate on your device. We do not access your camera or photos in the background or for any purpose outside the feature you're using.

Sharing your data

The App does not share or sell your estimate data, customer information, or photos. The only information shared with third parties is the limited subscription information sent to Google Play and RevenueCat to provide the subscription, as described in "Subscriptions and payments." If you use the App's "Export" or "Share" feature, you are choosing to send an estimate file yourself (for example, via email or a messaging app you select) — that action is initiated by you, not by us.

Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, only for the subscription feature:

  • Google Play Billing — to process subscription purchases (Google's terms and privacy policy apply to payments).
  • RevenueCat — to manage and validate subscriptions (see "Subscriptions and payments" above).

The App's typeface is bundled with the App, so it does not request fonts or other resources from the network. No advertising SDKs or analytics services are used beyond the subscription providers named above.

Data retention and deletion

You control your estimate data. Estimates can be deleted at any time from within the App (deleted estimates are held briefly in a "Recently Deleted" area so you can undo an accidental deletion, then permanently removed). Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. To request deletion of subscription-related data held by our subscription providers, contact us at the email below and we will assist.

Children's privacy

The App is a business tool intended for HVAC service professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date.
