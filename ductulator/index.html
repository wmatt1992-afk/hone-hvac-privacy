HONE Ductulator — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

HONE Ductulator ("the app") is published by HONE Builds. The app is a duct-sizing slide rule. It works entirely on your device: it needs no account, asks for no personal information, and the instrument itself never sends data anywhere.

Information the app itself stores

Your unit choice (Imperial or SI), the last position of the scales, and (on Android) whether you have purchased ad removal are stored locally on your device so the app opens where you left it. This information never leaves your device except as described below.

Advertising (Android version)

The Android version shows one small banner advertisement served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, app usage related to the ad, and crash diagnostics. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent message before loading ads; you can change your choice later through the "Ad choices" link in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Advertising (Windows version)

The Windows version is free and supported by advertising. It offers no ad-removal purchase: the advertising panels are a permanent part of the free desktop app.

The Windows version reserves two panels beside the instrument for advertising. At the time of writing no advertising network is enabled: the panels show no advertisements and request nothing. When advertising is switched on it will be one of the following, and this policy will be updated to name the provider before that happens:

  • Direct sponsorships — a still image supplied by a sponsor, linking to the sponsor's website. The image is loaded from our own website; nothing about you is sent to the sponsor unless you choose to open the link.
  • An advertising network, loaded inside an isolated frame. Such a network may process device and network information (such as your IP address and browser details) to serve and measure ads; the provider and its privacy policy, and how to opt out of interest-based advertising with that provider, will be listed here.

On start-up the Windows version fetches a small configuration file from our website (GitHub Pages) that controls these panels; the hosting service may log the request (IP address, time). The app never reads the Windows advertising ID.

Purchases (Android version only)

The Windows version has no purchases of any kind. On Android, ad removal is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; the app never sees your payment details. Purchase status is verified through RevenueCat, which receives the purchase token and an anonymous app-user identifier so ownership can be restored after a reinstall. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Once ad removal is verified, the app stops requesting advertisements entirely.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-in, no account, no email collection.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.
  • No analytics SDK of its own and no sale of personal information.

Children

The app is a professional trade tool and is not directed at children under 13.
