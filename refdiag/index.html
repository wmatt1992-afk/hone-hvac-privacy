HONE RefDiag — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

HONE RefDiag does not collect, transmit, or share anything you type into it. Every diagnosis is computed on your device. The only thing the app ever sends anywhere is the minimum needed to process and verify an in-app purchase, described below.

The short version: your readings, your diagnoses and your saved sessions never leave your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. The one exception is the purchase system: the Android app uses RevenueCat to handle its one-time unlock, and that is the only data that ever goes out.

What we collect

Diagnostic data: nothing. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no analytics or crash reporting SDK. It does not read your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files, and it never transmits a reading, a result or a saved session.

Purchase data (Android only): to sell and verify the one-time unlock, the Android app includes the RevenueCat SDK, which acts as our payment processor. When the app starts it generates an anonymous app-user identifier, and when you buy or restore the unlock it sends your Google Play purchase history for this app, that identifier, and the device identifiers RevenueCat uses to attribute and protect purchases, to RevenueCat's servers. This is used solely to confirm that your purchase is valid and to restore it on a new phone. We do not receive your name, email, or payment card details; Google Play handles the payment itself. RevenueCat's handling of this data is governed by its own privacy policy. Data is encrypted in transit. You can ask us to delete your purchase record by emailing the address below.

The Windows version of HONE RefDiag has no purchase system and sends nothing at all.

Data stored on your device

Diagnostic sessions you save, and your app preferences, are written to your device's local storage so the app can show them to you again. This information:

  • never leaves the device;
  • is not transmitted to us or to any third party;
  • is not accessible to us in any form;
  • is deleted when you delete the saved session, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Under Google Play's definitions, data that never leaves the device is not "collected" or "shared." We mention it here anyway so you know exactly what the app does.

Network access

The app performs its diagnosis offline using a knowledge base bundled inside the app itself. The diagnostic engine makes no HTTP requests and loads nothing from the internet at runtime. It works with the phone in airplane mode, which is deliberate — it is built to be used in mechanical rooms and basements with no signal. The only network use is the purchase system described above, and a purchase you have already made keeps working offline indefinitely.

Payments

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play and verified through RevenueCat. The app never sees, receives, or stores your payment card details; Google Play's own privacy policy governs the payment transaction, and RevenueCat's governs purchase verification.

Children

HONE RefDiag is a professional tool for HVAC and refrigeration technicians. It is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Changes are expected to be rare.
