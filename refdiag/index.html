HONE RefDiag — Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

HONE RefDiag does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device and makes no network requests.

The short version: nothing you type into this app leaves your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no servers to send anything to.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no analytics or crash reporting SDK. It does not read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Diagnostic sessions you save, and your app preferences, are written to your device's local storage so the app can show them to you again. This information:

  • never leaves the device;
  • is not transmitted to us or to any third party;
  • is not accessible to us in any form;
  • is deleted when you delete the saved session, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Under Google Play's definitions, data that never leaves the device is not "collected" or "shared." We mention it here anyway so you know exactly what the app does.

Network access

The app performs its diagnosis offline using a knowledge base bundled inside the app itself. It makes no HTTP requests, opens no sockets, and loads nothing from the internet at runtime. It works with the phone in airplane mode, which is deliberate — it is built to be used in mechanical rooms and basements with no signal.

Payments

If a paid tier is offered in a future version, purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees, receives, or stores your payment details. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

HONE RefDiag is a professional tool for HVAC and refrigeration technicians. It is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: honebuilds@gmail.com
